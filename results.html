Highest total net points wins. Stage positions use raw elapsed time. FINISH net points are the greater of (a) position points minus penalty deductions, or (b) the DNF score for clearing Gate 1, calculated as round(dynamic DNF base ÷ total gates). DNF points are round(dynamic DNF base × gates cleared ÷ total gates), then penalty deductions are subtracted; minimum 0. Total gate markers are set per SS in Race Control. DNS = 0. Default point deductions: Touch 15, Gate violation 5, Roll-over 5; the organiser may configure them before racing.
